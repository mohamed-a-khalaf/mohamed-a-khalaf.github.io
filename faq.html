Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping

Do You deliver to Australia ?

Yes we have worldwide delivery for an additional cost.

How long does it take to ship to HK?

Shipping to HK takes about 3 business weeks. Please allow up to 5 business weeks to account for customs clearance and other potential delays.

How much does delivery cost?

Order above $80 qualify for free delivery, otherwise shipping costs will be calculated with your quote.

Returns

Can I return my order if I don't like it?

Of course! You can return your order withing 1 week if you don't like it.

How do I initiate a return?

Please contact us by clicking here so we can send you a return label.

Orders

I want to cancel my order

If you have not yet received your order, simply refuse to receive it and the courier will return it back to us. You will only be charged the delivery fee.

Can I send you a custom design?

Yes, you can send custom designs and we will send you an estimate based on design complexity and other potential charges.

Is Cash On Delivery available?

Unfortunately COD is not currently available, but we you can pay using the bank transfer option. The quote you receive will have payment instructions for customers who prefer bank transfers.
